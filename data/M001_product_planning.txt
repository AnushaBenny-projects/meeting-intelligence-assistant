Meeting ID: M001
Meeting Title: Product Planning Meeting
Date: 2026-09-10
Participants: Anusha Rao, Sarah Patel, John Miller, Maya Chen, Priya Nair

Discussion:
- The team reviewed onboarding requirements for the new customer portal.
- Authentication was discussed across password login, SSO, and token-based APIs.
- Sarah Patel recommended JWT, also called JSON Web Token authentication, for version 1 because it works with the mobile app and backend API gateway.
- Priya Nair noted that SSO can be revisited after the enterprise pilot.

Decisions:
- The team decided to use JWT authentication for version 1 of the customer portal.
- The API will expose login, refresh-token, and logout endpoints.
- SSO is out of scope for the first release.
